# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming space for anyone interested in making AI agents work better on the web — regardless of background, experience level, or what AI tools they use.

## Our Standards

**Encouraged**:
- Specific, technical feedback grounded in evidence
- Questions, even basic ones
- Disagreement focused on ideas, not people
- Sharing experiments, benchmarks, and failure cases

**Not acceptable**:
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Harassment, public or private
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Sustained off-topic content
- Content designed to harm competing projects or individuals

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may remove comments, close issues, block contributors, or take other action for any behavior deemed inconsistent with this Code of Conduct.

Enforcement decisions are final.

## Reporting

Report problems by emailing <pk102h@naver.com>. Reports are handled confidentially. If your concern involves the maintainer, please still write; we will find a neutral third party to help.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces (GitHub issues, PRs, discussions, the IETF draft mailing lists for this document, and anywhere the project is officially represented).
